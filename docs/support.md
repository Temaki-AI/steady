# Steady Habits Support

Need help or found a bug? [Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/Temaki-AI/steady/issues)
and we'll take a look.

## Common questions

**My habits aren't syncing between devices.**
Make sure you're signed in to the same iCloud account on each device and that iCloud is
turned on for Steady Habits (iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud). Sync can take a minute.

**How do I add the widget?**
Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap **Edit → Add Widget**, search for **Steady Habits**,
and choose a size. You can check off habits right from the medium and large widgets.

**I'm not getting reminders.**
Check that notifications are allowed for Steady Habits in iOS Settings → Notifications.
Reminders are refreshed each time you open the app.

**How do "times per week" habits work?**
They stay due until you hit your target for the week (or month). After that they rest
until the next period, and your streak counts weeks or months where you hit the target.

**How do I export or delete my data?**
In the app, go to **Settings → Data**. See the [Privacy Policy](../privacy/) for details.
