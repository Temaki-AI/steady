# Privacy Policy

_Effective October 12, 2026_

Steady Habits is built to keep your data yours. **We do not collect any data.**

## What the app stores, and where

Your habits, check-offs and daily notes are stored:

- **On your device**, and
- **In your own private iCloud account**, if you're signed in to iCloud, so they sync
  between your devices. This uses Apple's CloudKit private database. Only you can read
  it; the developer has no access to it.

Nothing is sent to us or to any other server we operate.

## What we don't do

- No analytics, crash reporting, or usage tracking
- No advertising or ad identifiers
- No accounts or sign-up
- No third-party SDKs
- No selling or sharing of data, because we never receive any

## Reminders

Habit reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. They are not sent
through any server.

## Exporting and deleting your data

- **Export:** Settings → Data → Export as JSON or CSV. The file is created on your device
  and shared only where you choose to send it.
- **Delete:** Settings → Data → Erase All Data removes your habits, check-offs and notes
  from the app. Deleting the app removes its on-device data. To remove the copy stored
  in your iCloud account, go to iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage.

## Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new
effective date.
