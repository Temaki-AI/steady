## Description (4000)
Steady Habits is a calm, private habit tracker that helps you show up a little every day.

No ads. No accounts. No tracking. Just your habits.

CHECK OFF IN ONE TAP
Organize habits into morning, afternoon, evening and anytime. Tap to check off, with gentle haptics that make every win feel good.

SCHEDULES THAT FIT REAL LIFE
• Every day
• Specific days, like Mon/Wed/Fri
• A few times per week or month: rest days never break your streak
• Every few days

SEE YOUR PROGRESS
Streaks, completion rates and a contribution heatmap show how far you've come, for every habit and overall.

HOME SCREEN WIDGETS
See today at a glance and check off habits right from your Home Screen.

GENTLE REMINDERS
Up to three reminders per habit, and they stay quiet once you're done for the day.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your data stays on your device and in your own iCloud account, synced across your iPhones. We can't see it, and we never collect anything.

ALSO INCLUDED
• Daily notes to reflect on your day
• Export to JSON or CSV anytime
• Light and dark mode, plus accent colors
• Built-in accessibility support

Steady Habits is free and open source.
